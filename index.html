Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Here are the most common questions we get from our customers.

How often should I get my vehicle serviced?
It is generally recommended to have your vehicle serviced every 6 months or every 8,000 to 12,000 kilometres, depending on the manufacturer's guidelines.
What is included in a vehicle maintenance service?
Vehicle maintenance services typically include oil and filter changes, tyre rotations, fluid checks, and inspection of various components for wear and tear.
How do I know if my car needs a repair?
If you notice any unusual sounds, vibrations, warning lights, or a decrease in performance, it's a good indication that your car may need a repair.
Can you service vehicles from all manufacturers?
Yes, our experienced mechanics are trained to service vehicles from a wide range of manufacturers, ensuring reliable repairs and maintenance.
How long does an engine repair usually take?
The duration of an engine repair depends on the complexity of the issue. Simple repairs can be completed within a few hours, while major repairs may take longer.
What is involved in a brake system inspection?
A brake system inspection involves checking brake pads, rotors, callipers, brake lines, and fluid levels to ensure proper functioning and identify any issues.
How often should I replace my car's air filter?
Air filters should be replaced every 20,000 to 24,000 kilometres or as recommended by the vehicle manufacturer to maintain optimal engine performance.
Can you diagnose and fix electrical issues in vehicles?
Yes, our skilled technicians are trained to diagnose and repair various electrical issues, including faulty wiring, battery problems, and malfunctioning components.
Do you offer warranty on your repairs?
Yes, we stand behind the quality of our repairs and offer warranty coverage on parts and labour to provide our customers with peace of mind.
Can I schedule a service appointment online?
Absolutely! You can easily schedule a service appointment on our website or give us a call, and our friendly staff will assist you in booking a convenient time.
